# Midnight & Emerald

Use a bright Pearl White canvas, Midnight Navy text and Emerald Teal as the principal accent. Charts and tables should retain clear labels for group, eligibility and units. Reserve additional colors for meaningful comparisons, selection or support flags.

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight Navy | `#14283F` | Titles, text and strong comparison marks |
| Emerald Teal | `#177F78` | Main metric and series accent |
| Champagne Gold | `#D5A34A` | Select highlights and qualified observations |
| Muted Coral | `#C76B63` | Sparse or excluded support indicators paired with text |
| Slate Blue | `#71889B` | Secondary series and contextual marks |
| Pearl White | `#F5F7FA` | Bright canvas and light surfaces |

The [theme resource](P003_Bank_Campaign_Intelligence.Report/StaticResources/RegisteredResources/MidnightEmerald.json) is registered in the PBIR. Verify theme application in Desktop during handoff. Use Navy for small text on Pearl White and pair accent colors with readable labels so color alone carries no meaning.
